# Third-party software

QuangTV includes MIT-licensed TV Viewer source and the GPL-3.0-or-later NewPipeExtractor v0.26.5 for public YouTube extraction. The combined APK incorporating NewPipeExtractor is distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later; corresponding integration source and build instructions are in this repository. Original MIT copyright notices remain applicable to their files. NewPipe's license is in COPYING.NewPipe.

NewPipeExtractor: https://github.com/TeamNewPipe/NewPipeExtractor/tree/v0.26.5

No account cookies, remote extraction service, FFmpeg binaries, or YouTube account credentials are embedded in the extractor integration. Public, unrestricted videos only. Native Android MediaMuxer combines supported AVC/AAC tracks into MP4. Actual availability depends on YouTube's responses and must be verified on the user's network; a synthetic media test does not prove live YouTube extraction.
